3 Smart Rules for Safe AI Use

AI chatbots can be helpful for learning, but remember these basic safety rules:

1

AI bots are programs, not real human friends

Chatbots can sound friendly, but they do not have real feelings and store everything you write into computer memory.

2

Never share personal identity details

Keep your full name, home address, school, phone number, and passwords private. If NetWatch warns you, click ✨ Sanitize Prompt.

3

Talk to a real person if something feels wrong

If an AI bot or website ever makes you feel uncomfortable, confused, or sad, close the tab and talk to a parent or teacher!
